# gta6-game — Vice City Nights

Entry for the [make.fun `gta6-01` bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01):
build the most complete, highest-quality open-world crime game you can,
in the spirit of GTA 6 — an original sun-soaked city to explore on foot
and by car, with traffic and pedestrians, cars to steal, a wanted level
with police who respond, missions and a story, combat, a HUD with a
minimap, a start screen and a save. Playable in desktop Chrome from
static files, keyboard and mouse, no network request.

All content is original: own city, characters, vehicles, names, story,
art, sounds and music. Nothing from Rockstar Games' Grand Theft Auto
series or any other commercial game.
